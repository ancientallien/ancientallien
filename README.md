### 👋 Hey, I'm Abhishek

I'm a software engineer who loves turning **AI ideas into real, working products**. I studied
**Data Science and Biological Science at IIT Madras**, so I'm happiest where code meets something
bigger, whether that's helping people do their work faster or using AI to understand biology and health.

- 🤖 **What I do:** build apps powered by **LLMs, AI agents, and RAG**, from the first prototype to production
- 🧠 **What excites me right now:** agents that use tools, **MCP**, prompt optimization with **DSPy**, and figuring out how to *evaluate* LLMs properly
- 🧬 **Where I came from:** my thesis used deep learning (**Swin-UNET**, **UNET**) to detect breast cancer metastases, and I still love **AI for healthcare** and **computational biology**
- 🛠️ **How I like to work:** small prototype → real users → iterate. I care about the boring parts too: deployment, infra, and monitoring
- 💬 **Ask me about:** RAG, AI agents, text-to-SQL, vector databases, or taking an LLM demo to production
